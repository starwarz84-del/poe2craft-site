Welcome to PoE 2 Craft

Step-by-step crafting recipes and a crafting emulator for Path of Exile 2.

Why you can trust the numbers

Calculated, not guessed

Every probability comes from the game's own modifier weights. No AI or human picks a number.

Unknown stays unknown

When a weight isn't public, the step says so (weight unknown or estimated) instead of showing a made-up chance.

Checked by simulation

Recipe flowcharts are rolled tens of thousands of times with the same weights, so averages and worst cases are real outcomes.

Official wording

Item and modifier text is the game's own — English client text and Kakao Games' official Korean translation.

Recipes from real crafts

Each recipe follows a crafter's actual video, step by step, then gets its odds and cost calculated.

Cheap first

Recipes are ordered to spend as few expensive omens as possible, and say when to stop because a roll is already good enough.

How to use this website

  1. — follow a proven craft step by step, with the chance and cost of every step.
  2. — paste an item's text (Ctrl+C in game) to get a draft recipe for it.
  3. — pick a base and use currency on it exactly like in game. The odds of the next result are listed before you click.
  4. — pick the modifiers you want and see how rare each tier is.
  5. — special bases and crafting data, and which recipes use them.

Tabs keep your work while you switch between them. Language can be switched at the top right at any time.
